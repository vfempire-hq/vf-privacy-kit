## Who needs the commercial licence

You need the commercial licence if you want to use `vf-privacy-kit` in a
product where the AGPL-3.0 terms conflict with your distribution model:

- **Closed-source SaaS.** You run a network service that includes

  with your users, as AGPL-3.0 section 13 requires.
- **Proprietary desktop or mobile applications.** You distribute a

  the surrounding source under AGPL terms.
- **OEM / embedded distribution.** You embed `vf-privacy-kit` in a device

If you build fully open-source software under an AGPL-compatible licence,
you do not need the commercial licence — the AGPL-3.0 grant is all you
need.
